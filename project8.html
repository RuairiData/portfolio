Project 8 — Approvals Workflow Matrix (Power BI)

Power BI • matrix design • workflow visibility • governance

Why it’s relevant for BI / ops reporting

This matrix design makes workflow ownership and status instantly legible: task category/subcategory and approval type on the left, approvers across the top, with a simple Active/Inactive encoding that supports operational governance.

What I’d call out in interviews: designing matrices for scanning (not just reporting), making exceptions obvious, and choosing encodings that reduce cognitive load.
